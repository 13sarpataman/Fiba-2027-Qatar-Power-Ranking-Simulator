🧮 FIBA Ranking Points Formula

Loser: G = 10 × Region Factor × Stage Factor
Winner: GW = G × 1.25 × Opponent Factor × Away Factor × Margin Factor
Opponent Factor: 1 + (opponent's pre-game rating × 0.0001)
Away Factor: Home = 1.00 · Away = 1.10
Margin Factor: <15 = 1.00 · 15–29 = 1.05 · 30< = 1.20
Stage Factor: World Cup Qualifier second round = 1.20
Region Factor: Europe = 1.00 · Americas = 0.93 · Asia/Oceania = 0.68 · Africa = 0.69

Every game is calculated sequentially: the opponent's rating is the rating immediately before that game.
